Reference / Nodes

Unpose

Moves your weighted mesh from the pose of the fitted skeleton back onto the Arma rest pose.

Weight Transfer and Weight Cleanup weight your mesh in the pose of the fitted skeleton, the one Rig Fit made inside your mesh. Arma needs the mesh in its own rest pose. This node moves every point back: it applies the inverse of the bone matrices that carry the Arma skeleton to the fitted one, blended with the weights of the point. The weights are not changed.

Connect your weighted mesh to input 1, the output of Rig Fit as it is to input 2 (its fitted skeleton is used) and the Arma skeleton to input 3. The move of each bone is worked out from the joint positions only: how the bone turned, and how much longer or shorter it is along the bone (a longer arm is not a thicker arm). A perfect fit moves nothing. The message gives the mean and the largest move of the points, and the bones whose points moved most. The Arma rest pose has the pelvis at the origin, so these moves include the walk from where your character stands to there, about a metre.

A rigid piece (a helmet or a pouch made by Accessory Weights in Rigid mode) is moved by one matrix for all its points, so it keeps its shape. The unpose can only remove what the fitted skeleton describes: a landmark that is a few centimetres off in Rig Fit leaves the mesh a few centimetres off here.

Houdini namea3ob_unpose
Where to find itTab menu, A3OB, Unpose
Inputs3

Inputs

  1. Input 1: Your mesh. Your mesh with its cleaned bone weights, for example from Weight Cleanup.
  2. Input 2: Fitted skeleton. The output of Rig Fit as it is (its fitted skeleton is used).
  3. Input 3: Arma skeleton. The Arma skeleton, for example from Rig Skeleton. It must have the same bones as the fitted one.

Parameters

Parameters are in the order they appear on the node.

Rigid Pieces Keep Their Shape rigid_pieces

Move all the points of a rigid piece (made by Accessory Weights in Rigid mode) with one matrix, so the piece keeps its shape. Off: every point uses its own weights, which can stretch a rigid piece when its bone bends.

Type
On or off
Default
On

Group group

The points to unpose. Empty means all points. The menu lists the point groups of the input and shows the P3D name of a selection; picking adds or removes the group. The arrow button picks points in the viewport. You can also type group names, patterns or point numbers (for example 0-3).

Type
Group picker (text; the menu lists the point groups of the input, the arrow button picks in the viewport)
Default
(empty)
