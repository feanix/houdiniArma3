Reference / Nodes

Rig Fit

Fits the Arma skeleton and the sample body to your mesh, from landmarks that you set or that are guessed.

Your own character has its own size and proportions, and the Arma skeleton and the sample body do not match it. This node fits them to your mesh, so that Weight Transfer can copy the weights of the sample body onto your mesh, and Unpose can later bring your mesh back to the Arma rest pose. The Arma skeleton itself is never changed: the fitted skeleton is only a working copy.

The fit starts from landmarks, 18 named points of the body. Your mesh should stand upright (Y up), face +Z like the sample, and be in an A-pose with the arms away from the body. Set the landmarks with a Rig Landmarks node in front of this one: you drag a marker for each in the viewport. The chin and the groin are points on the skin (the chin is the tip, the groin the highest point between the legs); the others are joints, inside the body. The hands have two each: the last joint of the middle finger gives the length and the direction of the hand, the last joint of the thumb says which way the palm faces; the other fingers follow the hand. Left is the +X side. Landmarks that Rig Landmarks does not set are guessed from the shape of the mesh, and a position you set always wins over the guess. The guess is a first try: show the landmarks and correct the ones that are wrong.

The fit goes down the skeleton from the pelvis. Each joint is turned towards the landmarks below it and given the length that puts them on the landmarks. The message of the node says how far the joints were from their landmarks after the fit, and warns when that is more than 1 cm. The node has one output that holds three results as packed primitives, named by the prim string a3ob_rig_part: fitted_skeleton, fitted_body (the sample body posed to match your mesh, with its weights) and landmarks (points with a name). Connect it to input 2 of Weight Transfer as it is: Weight Transfer takes the fitted body from it. The Show menu only decides which results are drawn in the viewport; the output is the same for every choice. Refine (on by default) wraps the fitted body onto the points of the Body Group of your mesh with Topo Transfer, so the fitted body takes your proportions; the pelvis is placed from the torso, not from the groin.

Inputs3

Inputs

  1. Input 1: Your mesh. Your character in an A-pose, standing upright (Y up) and facing +Z like the sample. It may have landmark positions from a Rig Landmarks node in front of this one (see Landmarks).
  2. Input 2: Skeleton. The Arma skeleton, for example from Rig Skeleton. It is not changed.
  3. Input 3: Sample body. The sample body with its bone weights, for example Resolution 1 of the sample character from Extract LOD. Its bones must be the ones of the skeleton.

Parameters

Parameters are in the order they appear on the node.

Show show

Which results are drawn in the viewport. The node always gives all three results as one geometry, three packed primitives named by the prim string a3ob_rig_part. This menu only hides the others from view; the output is the same for every choice. Weight Transfer takes the fitted sample body from it by itself.

Type
Menu
Default
All

Choices:

  • All all: Draw the fitted skeleton, the fitted sample body and the landmarks.
  • Fitted skeleton skeleton: Draw the skeleton moved into your mesh (fitted_skeleton, a working copy, A'). Its joints are at your landmarks and its transform is a pure rotation (no scale) that aims the X axis down the bone to its child, with Y toward the front (+Z).
  • Fitted sample body body: Draw the sample body, posed and sized to match your mesh (fitted_body, S'). It keeps its bone weights.
  • Landmarks landmarks: Draw the landmarks that were used, as points with a name (landmarks).

Landmarks landmarks

Where the landmarks come from: the chin, the groin and the shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle, middle finger and thumb of each side. The Rig Landmarks node in front of this one holds the positions you placed (its markers are dragged in the viewport). Left is the +X side.

Type
Menu
Default
Both

Choices:

  • Auto-guess guess: Guess all landmarks from the shape of your mesh. Rig Landmarks is ignored.
  • From Rig Landmarks landmarks: Use only the positions of Rig Landmarks. All of them must be set.
  • Both both: Use the positions that are set in Rig Landmarks; guess the others. A position you set always wins over the guess.

Snap to Surface snap

Move the chin and the groin onto the closest point of your mesh. The joints lie inside the body and are never moved.

Type
On or off
Default
On

Symmetry symmetry

Make the left and right landmarks mirror each other. When both sides of a pair are set in Rig Landmarks they are averaged; when only one side is, the other side is its mirror image.

Type
On or off
Default
On

Refine refine

Wrap the fitted sample body onto your bare body with Topo Transfer, so it takes your proportions. Each landmark is moved to the closest point of the sample body and of your body; the pairs with the same name guide the wrap. The fitted sample body part becomes the wrapped body, with its bone weights. Off: the sample body is only posed and sized.

Type
On or off
Default
On

Body Group bodygroup

The point group of your mesh that Refine wraps onto: the bare body, without hair, helmet or other accessories. Empty means the whole mesh.

Type
Group picker (text; the menu lists the point groups of the input, the arrow button picks in the viewport)
Default
body

Scale scale

How the length of each bone is fitted.

Type
Menu
Default
Per bone length

Choices:

  • Per bone length bone: Each bone gets the length that puts its end joint on the landmark.
  • Whole body body: One size for the whole skeleton. The joints are turned towards the landmarks but not moved onto them, so the quality shows how far off they are.
