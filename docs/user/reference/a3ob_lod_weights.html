Reference / Nodes

LOD Weights

Gives a reduced LOD the bone weights of the finished Res 1: each point takes the weights of the closest place on Res 1.

PolyReduce moves and merges points, so the weights it carries along no longer add up to 1. This node weights a reduced LOD again from the finished Res 1: each point takes the weights of the closest place on the Res 1 surface, interpolated across the Res 1 triangle, then limited to 4 bones and normalised like Weight Cleanup does.

A rigid piece of Res 1 (a helmet or a plate from Accessory Weights in Rigid mode, or an island that follows one bone only) keeps its weights exactly: a point next to it takes the piece's weights, with no blend. A point farther from Res 1 than Match Distance is listed in a warning and filled in from its neighbours. The bone selections of input 1 are replaced; its other groups and attributes stay. The points get a3ob_rig_origin (1 copied, 2 filled in, 3 rigid piece). LOD chain: Unpose, PolyReduce, LOD Weights (input 2 is the weighted Res 1), LOD Properties.

Houdini namea3ob_lod_weights
Where to find itTab menu, A3OB, LOD Weights
Inputs3

Inputs

  1. Input 1: LOD to weight. The LOD that gets the weights, for example Res 1 after PolyReduce. Its own bone selections are replaced; its other groups and attributes stay.
  2. Input 2: Weighted source (Res 1). The source with bone weights (point groups with a3ob_w weights), usually Res 1 after Unpose and the weight nodes. Rigid pieces (a3ob_rig_piece) keep their weights.
  3. Input 3: Skeleton. The skeleton, for example from Rig Skeleton. Its joint names decide which selections are bones and how they are spelled. If its model.cfg class has isDiscrete = 1, every point keeps only its heaviest bone.

Parameters

Parameters are in the order they appear on the node.

Match Distance distance

How far from the source a point may be and still copy its weights, as a share of the size of the LOD (the diagonal of its bounding box). Farther points are listed and filled in from their neighbours.

Type
Number
Default
0.05
Allowed
At least 0; no upper limit

Max Bones per Point maxbones

The most bones one point may have. Arma allows 4. The lightest weights beyond this are dropped.

Type
Whole number
Default
4
Allowed
From 1 to 16

Prune Below threshold

Weights below this are dropped. A point never loses its heaviest weight.

Type
Number
Default
0.01
Allowed
From 0 to 1
