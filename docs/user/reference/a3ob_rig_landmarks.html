Reference / Nodes

Rig Landmarks

Places the landmarks of your character (chin, groin, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles) for Rig Fit, as positions that you drag in the viewport.

Rig Fit fits the skeleton to 14 landmarks of your character: the chin, the groin, and the left and right shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee and ankle. This node holds them as positions (X, Y, Z) that you place by hand. Put the node between your mesh and Rig Fit. Connect the skeleton and the sample body to its inputs 2 and 3 (the ones that go into Rig Fit) and press Guess Landmarks: it fills all 14 fields with the guess of Rig Fit. Then select the node, move the mouse over the viewport and press Enter: every landmark is a labelled marker that you drag. The markers are drawn on top of the mesh. A joint belongs inside the body; the chin and the groin are on the skin.

Mirror is on by default. Left is the +X side. The right markers follow the left ones, mirrored about the X = 0 plane, so dragging a left marker moves its right partner (the right fields cannot be edited then). Switch Mirror off to place both sides freely. To put a landmark back to the guess, choose it in the Landmark menu and press Guess This Landmark. To set a landmark from the mesh, select points in the viewport, choose the landmark in the Landmark menu and press Set From Selected Points: it takes the average of the points. Only the position is kept, not the selection. This is handy for the chin and the groin.

The node gives your mesh unchanged, with one detail vector per landmark that is set (a3ob_lm_chin and so on), which Rig Fit reads. A fresh node has no position set (all three numbers zero means not set); it warns and needs the guess. Rig Fit then guesses the landmarks that are not set.

Houdini namea3ob_rig_landmarks
Where to find itTab menu, A3OB, Rig Landmarks
Inputs1 to 3

Inputs

  1. Input 1: Your mesh. Your character mesh, the one that goes into Rig Fit. It comes out unchanged.
  2. Input 2: Skeleton. Optional. The Arma skeleton, for Guess Landmarks (the same one that goes into Rig Fit).
  3. Input 3: Sample body. Optional. The sample body with its bone weights, for Guess Landmarks (the same one that goes into Rig Fit).

Parameters

Parameters are in the order they appear on the node.

Guess Landmarks guess

Fills all 14 positions with the auto-guess of Rig Fit for your mesh. Connect the skeleton to input 2 and the sample body to input 3 first. It replaces the positions that are set.

Type
Button

Mirror mirror

Left is the +X side. On: the right landmarks follow the left ones, mirrored about the X = 0 plane, so dragging a left marker moves its right partner, and the right fields cannot be edited. Off: both sides are free.

Type

Default
On

Match Left to Right leftToRight

Once: puts each right landmark at the mirror image of its left partner (mirrored about X = 0, Left is +X). Chin and groin stay as they are, and a left landmark that is not set is skipped. Disabled while Mirror is on, because the right side already follows the left.

Type
Button
Editable when
Mirror is off

Match Right to Left rightToLeft

Once: puts each left landmark at the mirror image of its right partner (mirrored about X = 0, Left is +X). Chin and groin stay as they are, and a right landmark that is not set is skipped. With Mirror on, the right side then follows the new left side.

Type
Button
